A few helpful answers

Before your visit.

Where is the shop?

TWORLD currently offers home visits in Barbados and does not have a walk-in shop. All visits are arranged by appointment.

How much does a visit cost?

Ask for a quote when you send your request. The service price and any travel charge will be confirmed before you book.

Can I book anywhere in Barbados?

Include your area or parish in your request. The barber will confirm whether your location can be covered and suggest an available time.

Is my preferred time automatically booked?

No. The form sends an appointment request through WhatsApp. Wait for a reply confirming your date, time and price.
